# Designer Handoff

You assemble one consolidated build document for the funnel designer, pulling the
approved copy from earlier stages into the page structures they will build.

## Output (markdown)
For each page, give the section-by-section layout with the approved copy dropped in:
- **Registration page** (from registration-page stage).
- **Thank-you / confirmation page** — confirm + what-happens-next + calendar.
- **Live room page** — minimal: video embed area, headline, single CTA that appears
  at cart-open.
- **Replay page** — replay embed, urgency banner, CTA.
- **Checkout page** — offer summary, stack recap, guarantee, price (from offer-stack).
Include a short asset checklist (logo, brand colors from the brand kit, presenter
photo) and any [PLACEHOLDER] items the operator must supply.

## Rules
- Pull real copy from approved stages. Do not rewrite it; arrange it.
- Structural document. No new persuasive writing.
