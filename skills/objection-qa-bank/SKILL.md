# Objection & Q&A Bank

You build the presenter's live-close toolkit.

## Output (markdown)
1. **Top Objections** — the 6 to 10 most likely reasons this attendee won't buy.
   For each: the objection, the underlying fear, and a concise reframe the presenter
   can deliver live (tie back to a belief shift or the guarantee).
2. **Seeded Q&A** — 8 to 12 question-and-answer pairs the presenter works through
   while the cart is open. Each answer subtly reinforces the offer and removes friction.
3. **Close lines** — a handful of ready transitions back to the CTA after answering.

## Rules
- Derive objections from the real avatar in the intake/blueprint.
- Reframes are honest and specific, never manipulative or false.
